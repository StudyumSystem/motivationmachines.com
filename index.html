Establishing the biological baseline for artificial intelligence.

Initiative

Motivation Machines is an independent non-profit biotechnology and artificial intelligence research laboratory. Our mandate is to quantify the physiological primitives of human drive, cognitive endurance, and biological purpose.

While artificial intelligence has advanced dramatically in computational reasoning, a profound gap remains: these systems possess no contextual awareness of the human biological state. Our understanding of motivation has traditionally been confined to subjective psychology, restricting our ability to build intelligent systems that truly honor human limits and elevate our potential. Motivation Machines was established to resolve this disparity. We are translating the autonomic markers of human performance into a universally understood, computationally legible foundation.

We are a collective of behavioral scientists, neuroscientists, medical researchers, and biomechanical experts anchored by over four decades of foundational research.

Originating in the academic laboratories of the 1980s, our principal scientists have pioneered advancements across sports psychology, physiology, and neurodivergent cognitive development. From orchestrating profound neurological breakthroughs for neurodivergent learners through precise physical brain activation protocols, to capturing the high-stress telemetry of elite athletes, we have devoted a lifetime to mapping the elegant mechanics of human drive outside the constraints of a traditional laboratory.

Science is better when shared

Scientific advancement is inherently a collective endeavor. We believe the most effective path to expanding humanity's understanding of biological motivation is through graceful and open collaboration with the global community of researchers, educators, and builders. As an independent non-profit, our ultimate loyalty is to the public good. By publishing our foundational physiological datasets and behavioral models, we aim to enrich the global scientific community and continually refine the integrity of our own research.

Harmonizing AI with the human state

An emphasis on biological context. Rather than focusing exclusively on autonomous systems designed to operate independently of human beings, we are devoted to cultivating multimodal systems that work in respectful harmony with our biology. By interpreting the autonomic nervous system in real-time, we are laying the groundwork for technology that gracefully adapts to the unique cognitive and physiological state of the individual.

Solid foundations matter

High-fidelity telemetry as the cornerstone. Algorithmic intelligence is undeniably powerful, but it requires an uncompromising standard of biological data. We conduct our research at the absolute frontier of human capability, rooted in high-fidelity physiological telemetry.

Transforming billions of lives. Unlocking these advanced biological models will catalyze transformative applications, empowering billions of individuals to experience more peaceful, productive, and deeply fulfilling lives. Our work is fundamentally a contribution to the graceful advancement of humanity.
